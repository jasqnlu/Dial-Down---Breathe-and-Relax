so before i hop on i run through a few things. breathe in for 4 and out for 6, unclench my jaw, feet flat on the floor, a few slow sips of water

writing down three things i want to say is the one i'd never skip. even if the chat goes somewhere weird, i've got something to look at and i don't freeze

it's usually about nothing, honestly. my body just hasn't figured that out yet
