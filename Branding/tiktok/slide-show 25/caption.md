the little reset i do in the car before i walk in the door

**Caption**

some days i pull into the driveway and just sit there for a minute because i'm not ready to go inside yet

so i turned it into a thing. engine off, phone face down, hands in my lap. then shoulders up to my ears, hold it, and let them drop

the loud sigh is my favourite part. twice, on purpose, because nobody can hear me in there 😮‍💨

it takes about two minutes and i'm a lot less snappy with whoever's home when i get inside
