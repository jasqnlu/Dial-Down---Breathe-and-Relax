**Title** (TikTok photo-mode title, ≤ 90 characters)

the 2 minutes i steal between back to back calls

**Caption**

some days my calendar is one call after another with no gap, and by the third one i'm barely listening

so i steal two minutes in between. i stand up and push my chair back so i can't just sit straight back down, put my hands behind my head with my elbows wide for one big breath in, then fold forward and let my arms hang while i breathe out slowly

the fold is my favourite bit. hanging there with my head down is the only moment all afternoon that nobody can see my face

it's such a small thing and i still come back to the next call a bit less fried

try it before your next call
