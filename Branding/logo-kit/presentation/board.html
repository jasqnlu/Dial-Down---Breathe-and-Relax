What we heard

The brief

A breathing and stretching app for people who want a calm, short daily ritual. The old icon (a clock with a stick figure) said 'timer' and got noisy at small sizes; the new mark should say breath and stretch at a glance and hold up on the home screen.

calmwarmalivegentle

Success: reads as breath + stretch in one shape, keeps the app's amber equity, stays clear at 29 px, works in one colour and in iOS dark/tinted modes.

Concept A

Open Lungs · Airways

Two lung lobes that are also arms raised overhead: breath and stretch in one shape, with the airways branching out of the body.

  • One shape carries both halves of the name: lungs (breath) and a reaching figure (stretch)
  • Amber gradient rises bottom-to-top like an inhale and keeps the app's existing accent
  • Solid silhouette survives 29 px; airways add depth at icon and marketing sizes
  • Mirrored and built from one lobe curve, so it extends into patterns and motion (lobes can 'breathe')
