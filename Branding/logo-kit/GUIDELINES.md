# Dial Down — Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo

**Idea:** two lung lobes that are also arms raised overhead. Breath and stretch in one shape. The airways branch
out of the gap between the lobes, which doubles as the body. The amber gradient rises from the bottom like an inhale.

| Version | File | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal (primary) | `dialdown-horizontal.svg` / `-on-dark` | Website header, App Store marketing, press |
| Stacked | `dialdown-stacked.svg` / `-on-dark` | Square spaces: splash, social posts, merch |
| Symbol | `dialdown-symbol.svg` / `-on-dark` | Avatars, in-app badges, anywhere the name is already shown |
| Symbol, small cut | `dialdown-symbol-small.svg` | 32 px and below (favicon). Same silhouette without airways |
| Wordmark | `dialdown-wordmark.svg` | Rare. Only when the symbol already appears nearby |
| One colour | `*-black.svg`, `*-white.svg`, `dialdown-symbol-accent.svg` | Engraving, embroidery, single-ink print, overlays on photos |

All artwork is generated by `Tools/branding/build_logo_kit.py`. Change the geometry or palette there and
re-run it. Don't hand-edit the SVGs.

## 2. Clear space

Keep clear space of **1 × the head diameter** (the dot) on every side. It scales with the logo.

## 3. Minimum size

| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 120 px wide | 30 mm wide |
| Stacked | 64 px wide | 18 mm wide |
| Symbol (full) | 40 px | 10 mm |
| Symbol, small cut | 16 px | 5 mm |

## 4. Colour

| Name | Role | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ember | Gradient base, head on light | `#C75F0E` | 199 95 14 | 0 52 93 22 |
| Amber | Gradient middle | `#F59A3A` | 245 154 58 | 0 37 76 4 |
| Glow | Gradient top, tagline on dark | `#FFC978` | 255 201 120 | 0 21 53 0 |
| Cream | Head on dark | `#FFE2B0` | 255 226 176 | 0 11 31 0 |
| Accent | One-colour brand version, tagline on light | `#B5540A` | 181 84 10 | 0 54 95 29 |
| Ink | Dark background, wordmark on light | `#0E1413` | 14 20 19 | 30 0 5 92 |
| Paper | Light background, wordmark on dark | `#FFF4E6` | 255 244 230 | 0 4 10 0 |

Ink and Accent match the app's `luminaSurface` (dark) and `AccentColor` (light), so the icon and the UI stay one
system. The CMYK values are straight conversions. Proof them before a print run, and match Ember/Accent to a Pantone
guide under real light.

**Text contrast (WCAG):** Accent on white 4.96:1 (AA) · Ink on Paper 17:1 · Glow on Ink 12.3:1. Ember on white is only
4.1:1, so use it for the mark, not for small text.

**Approved pairs:** full colour on white or Paper · `-on-dark` version on Ink · white on Accent or Ember · black on
white. On photos, use the white or black version over a calm area.

## 5. Typography

- Wordmark "Dial Down" and tagline "BREATHE AND RELAX": **Nunito** (ExtraBold 750 / Bold 700, tagline tracked +200), outlined in the files.
  Open Font License, so it's free for logo use.
- In the app, keep using the system font. Nunito belongs to the logo, not the UI.

## 6. iOS app icon

`ios/icon-light.png`, `icon-dark.png` and `icon-tinted.png` are 1024 px and opaque (no alpha channel). They're already
installed in `AppIcon.appiconset`. The tinted version is white on black; iOS applies the user's tint to it.

## 7. Don'ts

Don't stretch, squash or rotate the logo. Don't recolour it outside the palette or flip the gradient. Don't add
shadows, outlines or glows around the mark (the glow on the dark app icon is part of that tile only). Don't rearrange
or resize the parts of a lockup. Don't use the full airway symbol below 40 px; use the small cut. Don't retype
"Dial Down" in a font.
