the wall check i do
every afternoon

heels, butt and

shoulders flat

against the wall

then see if your head

touches the wall too

chin level, not up

mine didn’t at first

so i tuck my chin

and slide it back

then i walk around

for a minute

before i sit back down

I also use the Dial Down app.
You tap the spot that’s tight
on a 3D body and it pulls up
stretches for that exact spot.
It’s free, no subscription.
You can get it on the App Store.

did your head touch?
