pov: your boss just sent
“got a sec for a quick chat?”
do this before you hop on

1. breathe out longer
in for 4, out for 6

2. unclench your jaw
tongue up, teeth apart

3. feet flat on the floor
feel the chair hold you

4. name 3 things you can see
gets you out of your head

5. drink some water
slow sips, not gulps

6. write down 3 things
you want to say

7. or let an app do it
Dial Down has box breathing
that says each step out loud
plus 4-7-8 and belly
it’s free, no subscription
it’s on the app store

save this for the next one
