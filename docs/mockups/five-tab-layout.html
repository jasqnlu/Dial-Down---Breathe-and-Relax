Dial Down · seperate-main · Proposal

Five tabs instead of six

People said the bottom bar has too many buttons. This proposal moves the Body map into the Exercises tab as a second view, so the bar drops from six tabs to five and no feature is lost.

−1

Body is no longer its own tab

The 3D body map was a way to find stretches for a sore spot. That's the same job as Exercises.

↔

Exercises gets an Areas / Body switch

Areas is today's bubble map, search and filters. Body is the 3D map, unchanged. The switch remembers your last choice.

✓

Everything else stays put

Today, Breathe, Routines and Profile keep their screens. Each tab gets about 20% more width, so icons are easier to tell apart and hit.

Try it: tap the tabs, and switch Areas / Body on Exercises.

The bottom bar

Same floating bar and pop-out pastel circle as now, with one fewer tab.

Build notes

Details to handle in the implementation.

Tab numbers shift

Tabs are picked by index in HomeView. Exercises goes from 2 → 1, Breathe 3 → 2, Routines 4 → 3, Profile 5 → 4. Update originTab: 4 in Routines and selectedTab = 2 for "browse exercises."

Links into Body

Anything that opened the Body tab switches to Exercises with the Body view selected, so existing entry points still land in the right place.

App tour

The tabbar.body tour stop goes away. Its step should point at the new Areas / Body switch.

UI tests

Tests that tap app.buttons["Body"] or launch with -debugInitialTab need the new indexes and a tap on the Body switch.

Body scene stays alive

Keep the 3D scene mounted when you flip to Areas, so switching back is instant and keeps your zoom and rotation.

Later: four tabs?

Today already shows pinned and premade routines. Routines could fold into Today later for a four-tab bar. That's a separate decision.
